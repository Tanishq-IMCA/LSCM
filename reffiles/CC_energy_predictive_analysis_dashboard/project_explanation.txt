Project: Solar Energy Consumption Prediction and Visualization Dashboard

**1. Project Overview:**

This project aims to develop a predictive analysis tool for solar energy consumption. The system will track energy generated by solar panels, consumption from the solar battery reserve, and the switch to the grid once the reserve is depleted. The core of the project is a web-based dashboard that visualizes historical energy usage, predicts future consumption patterns, and provides actionable insights to the user.

The project is divided into three main modules:
- **Module 1: Data Generation:** Creates realistic datasets for training a predictive model and for populating the dashboard.
- **Module 2: Model Training:** Uses the generated data to train a machine learning model capable of forecasting energy consumption.
- **Module 3: Dashboard and Prediction:** A React-based frontend that visualizes the data, displays predictions from the trained model, and allows for interactive data exploration.

**2. Module 1: Data Generation (`generator.py`)**

This module will be responsible for creating two CSV files:

*   `training_data.csv`: A large dataset for training the predictive model.
*   `dashboard_data.csv`: A dataset representing the "current" state of the system, which will be displayed on the dashboard.

**Data Columns:**

*   `timestamp`: The date and time of the energy reading (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`).
*   `energy_consumption_kwh`: The amount of energy consumed in kWh during that interval.
*   `source`: The source of the energy, which can be 'solar_battery' or 'grid'.
*   `solar_generation_kwh`: The amount of energy generated by the solar panels in kWh.
*   `battery_charge_percent`: The remaining charge of the solar battery reserve (0-100%).

**Realism in Data:**

The data generation will simulate real-world scenarios:

*   **Variable Consumption:** Energy usage will fluctuate based on the time of day, day of the week, and season. For example, higher consumption during evenings and weekends, and seasonal peaks (e.g., more AC usage in summer).
*   **Irregular Entries:** There will be multiple data points for a single day, reflecting continuous energy use. Some days may have no entries, simulating vacations or periods of very low activity.
*   **Solar Generation:** Solar energy generation will be dependent on the time of day (peaking midday) and weather conditions (less generation on cloudy days, which will be simulated).
*   **Battery Dynamics:** The battery will charge from solar generation and discharge from consumption. When the battery is depleted, the `source` will switch to 'grid'.

**3. Module 2: Model Training (`train_model.py`)**
This module will use the `training_data.csv` to train a predictive model. A time-series forecasting model like SARIMA or a machine learning model like a Random Forest Regressor or Gradient Boosting will be suitable.

The model will be trained to predict `energy_consumption_kwh` based on historical data, time of day, day of the week, and other relevant features. The trained model will be saved to a file (e.g., `saved_model.pkl`) for use by the backend API.

**4. Module 3: Dashboard and Prediction (React Frontend & Python Backend)**

This module consists of a Python backend (using Flask or FastAPI) and a React frontend.

**Backend (`app.py`):**

*   An API endpoint will load the trained model (`saved_model.pkl`).
*   It will take the `dashboard_data.csv` as input.
*   It will generate future predictions for energy consumption.
*   It will provide API endpoints for the frontend to fetch both historical data and predictions.

**Frontend (React):**

*   **Dashboard UI:** A modern, minimalistic dashboard with a glassmorphism design.
*   **Main Chart:**
    *   Displays historical energy consumption with a solid line.
    *   Overlays future predicted consumption with a dotted line.
    *   The color of the prediction line will change (e.g., to red) to indicate "danger zones," such as periods of high grid dependency or rapid battery depletion.
*   **Filters:** Buttons to filter the data view by Day, Week, and Month.
*   **Key Metrics:** Display of key metrics like:
    *   Current battery charge.
    *   Percentage of energy from solar vs. grid.
    *   Predicted time until battery depletion.
    *   Comparison of the current period's consumption with the previous period.
*   **Solar Generation Visualization:** A separate chart or visual element to show solar energy generation throughout the day.

**5. Predictive Features:**

The dashboard will provide the following predictions:

*   **Future Energy Consumption:** A forecast of kWh usage for the next day, week, or month.
*   **Battery Depletion:** Predict when the solar battery reserve is likely to run out based on current trends.
*   **Grid Dependency:** Forecast the amount of energy that will be drawn from the grid.
*   **Cost Analysis (Future Scope):** The system will be designed to easily incorporate electricity grid costs later on, allowing for financial predictions related to energy spending.

**6. Technology Stack:**

*   **Backend:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Flask/FastAPI
*   **Frontend:** React, Chart.js (or a similar charting library), CSS (for styling)
*   **Data:** CSV files
